A 3D maze game

Treasure Hunt

Find every coin in the temple maze to open the exit. Sentries guard the halls, but you can see exactly what they see, and every shot gives you time to dodge.

Levels

Controls

  • WASD or arrow keys: move
  • Shift: run · Space: dash
  • Q E or drag: turn the camera · wheel: zoom
  • P or Esc: pause · M: sound
  • Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb turns the camera, Dash button dashes
  • Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick turns, A dashes, Start pauses

What hurts, and what helps

  • Sentry sight. The lit cone on the floor is what a sentry sees. It also notices you close behind it.
  • Sentry orb, −15. The sentry's eye turns red and charges before it fires. Step aside, break line of sight, or dash through the orb.
  • Spike trap, −10. The plate glows orange, then the spikes rise.
  • Potion, +35 health.
